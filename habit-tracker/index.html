HABIT TRACKER

A habit tracker that knows the rest of your life.

Most habit trackers are a grid of checkboxes in an app of their own. The Organizr Tracker is a full habit tracker — daily check-ins, streaks, statistics — that lives in the same app as your workouts, meals, measurements and goals, so you can finally see how they affect each other.

HOW IT WORKS

Daily check-ins, real statistics

The Tracker is where the day gets recorded. What you build up there feeds the dashboards in every other section, and Organizr AI reads the same data when it looks for patterns.

Daily check-ins and streaks

Mark habits done as the day goes and keep the streak alive. Streaks are the cheapest motivation there is, and they are the first thing you see when the app opens.

Statistics that go past the grid

Beyond the checkboxes there is a dashboard: completion over weeks and months, the habits that hold and the ones that quietly stopped.

Habits next to workouts and meals

Because the workout log, the food diary and the body measurements are in the same app, a stalled habit and a bad training week stop being two unrelated facts. See the Workout section.

Check in from Apple Watch

Tick off a habit from your wrist without unlocking the phone. It lands in the same Tracker.

Habits that serve a goal

Goals run on three-month cycles or across a full year. Attach the habits that feed them, then track both in one place.

Yours, and encrypted

Sync keeps diary entries, completed workouts, meals and Time Analyzr blocks encrypted on the server for 90 days from the event date. Goals expire 90 days after their quarter ends. Complete Wallet history, Board, subscriptions, measurements, recipes, routines and settings are exceptions with no automatic expiry. Expiry removes only the server copy; data stays on your devices. Does it show streaks?

Yes. Every habit keeps a streak, and the dashboard shows completion trends over weeks and months alongside the rest of your data.

Can habits be linked to goals?

Yes. Goals in Organizr run on three-month cycles or across a full year, and the habits that feed a goal are tracked in the same app.

What makes it different from a standalone habit tracker?

Context. Habits sit next to your workout log, food diary, body measurements, goals and spending, so you can see what actually moves together instead of comparing four separate apps.
