FAQ

About the food diary

Related: habit tracker, workout tracker and wallet.

Do I have to type in every meal?

Not if you connect FatSecret. Meals, calories and nutritional values come across automatically, and the setup guide walks through creating the API credentials step by step.

Does the food diary track macronutrients?

Yes. Every entry carries calories and its macronutrient split, with daily totals and dashboards over time.

Is the food diary available on the free plan?

Yes. The food diary is included in Organizr Lite, along with up to 6 recipes. Pro and Lifetime remove the limits and add the advanced dashboards.

Can I save my own recipes?

Yes. You can create, save and organize personal recipes, plan meals with them, and have Organizr AI analyze their nutritional values or generate new ones.

Does it connect to my body measurements?

Yes. Weight, BMI and body measurements are tracked in the same app, so nutrition data and physical change sit on the same timeline.
