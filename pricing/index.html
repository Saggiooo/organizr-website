FAQ

Questions about plans

Is there a free version of Organizr?

Yes. Organizr Lite is free and includes the full Tracker, the food diary, one unlimited board, up to 3 workout routines, 6 recipes, 6 subscriptions, a wallet with one account, Apple Watch workouts and 50 Organizr AI credits per month.

What do I get with Organizr Pro?

Every section with no limits, advanced dashboards, sync across your devices, Time Analyzr, wardrobe and file organization on desktop, and 300 Organizr AI credits per month. Pro costs €3.49 per month or €17.99 per year.

What is the Lifetime plan?

A single payment of €49.99 for permanent access to every Pro feature, with no renewals. Organizr AI is included at 300 credits per month for 24 months.

Can I use Organizr on more than one device?

Sync keeps diary entries, completed workouts, meals and Time Analyzr blocks encrypted on the server for 90 days from the event date. Goals expire 90 days after their quarter ends. Complete Wallet history, Board, subscriptions, measurements, recipes, routines and settings are exceptions with no automatic expiry. Expiry removes only the server copy; data stays on your devices. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Which platforms is Organizr available on?

iPhone, Android, macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows 10 or 11, plus Apple Watch for logging workouts, spending and habits from your wrist. See the download page.

Can I pay in dollars?

Yes. Use the € / $ switch above the plans to see every price in US dollars.
