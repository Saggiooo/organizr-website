HEVY INTEGRATION

Hevy, synced in one click.

Connect your Hevy account and import the full workout history into Organizr. Routines, exercises, sets, reps, weights and records are pulled and kept aligned with a single Sync Hevy button.

  • Automatic import of every past session.
  • Exercises matched and deduplicated across routines.
  • Personal records detected and tagged.
  • Manual workouts can live next to Hevy ones — no lock-in.
  • Data stays local: Hevy is only the source, not a dependency.

COMPARE

Every set, against last time.

Open any workout and Organizr lines it up next to the previous session of the same routine, set by set. You see exactly what changed: weight, reps, volume.

  • Δ kg — load difference per set.
  • Δ reps — reps gained or lost.
  • Δ volume — kg × reps swing per set and per exercise.
  • Per-exercise total volume vs. previous session.
  • Set count comparison to catch missed or added sets.

PROGRESSION

Load progression, on a real chart.

For every exercise Organizr plots how the weight evolved over time. Filter by muscle group, equipment or source — Hevy or manual — and read the trend without exporting anything.

  • kg over time for any exercise, across the whole history.
  • Range stats: total sessions, total sets, last value, full delta.
  • Filters by tag (e.g. chest, smith, barbell) to compare similar lifts.
  • Toggle between recorded and manual data points.
  • Last-set series: one stable data point per workout, no noise.

HEATMAP

A year of training, at one glance.

Every workout becomes a cell on the calendar. Darker means a longer session — so you read consistency, gaps and peak weeks without opening a single chart.
