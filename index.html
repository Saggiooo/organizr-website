FAQ

Common questions.

What is Organizr?

Organizr is the all-in-one suite for personal growth on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows. It combines a habit tracker, a kanban board, a workout log, a food diary, body measurements, goals, a wallet, subscriptions, recipes, a wardrobe, a reading library and file organization, with every section sharing data with the others.

What is the difference between Organizr and the Board, Wallet and Workout apps?

Organizr is the complete suite with every section. Board, Wallet and Workout are dedicated iOS apps focused on one area, lighter and quicker to use. They share the same account and licence: with sync turned on, what you log in one app also appears in Organizr, on iPhone and on desktop.

Do I have to pay for each app separately?

No. One Premium plan covers Organizr and every dedicated app, on all your devices.

Which devices does Organizr run on?

iPhone, Android, macOS 12 or later and Windows 10 or 11. Apple Watch can log workouts, spending and habits from your wrist.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices. With sync on, data travels end-to-end encrypted: diary entries, completed workouts, meals and Time Analyzr blocks stay encrypted on the server for 90 days from the event date, while Wallet history, Board, subscriptions, measurements, recipes and routines do not expire. Expiry only removes the server copy. The Privacy Policy has the full list.

Can I import workouts from Hevy or meals from FatSecret?

Yes. Workout imports your Hevy history, so past sessions and load progression are there from day one, and the food diary can connect to FatSecret so meals and macros arrive automatically.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Organizr Lite is free and needs no account: full tracker, food diary, a board, workout routines, recipes, subscriptions, a wallet account and 50 Organizr AI credits a month.
