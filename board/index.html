FAQ

About Board

Related: Habit tracker, Workout tracker and Wallet.

Is Board free?

Yes. Board is free with one workspace, and Organizr Lite includes one unlimited board. Premium unlocks unlimited workspaces and covers Board and the full Organizr on every device.

What is the difference between the Board app and Organizr?

Board is a dedicated iPhone app focused only on your projects. Organizr includes the same Board next to every other section. They share one account and one licence, and with sync turned on the same tasks appear in both.

Can I see my tasks on a timeline?

Yes. Any task with a start and an end date appears on the Timeline, with a colour you can choose for each event.

Does Board sync across devices?

With Premium, Board syncs end-to-end encrypted with Organizr on iPhone, Mac and Windows. Board data on the server does not expire automatically.

Can Organizr AI create tasks for me?

Yes. Organizr AI can propose tasks and events from what you ask, and you decide what to add to the board.

Which devices does it run on?

The Board app runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Board is also part of Organizr on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows.
