FAQ

Before you install

Everything about plans is on the pricing page.

Is Organizr free to download?

Yes. Organizr Lite is free on every platform and includes the full Tracker, the food diary, a kanban board, workout routines, recipes, subscriptions and a wallet account. You can upgrade to Pro or Lifetime later from inside the app or from the pricing page.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 12 or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Pick the build that matches your chip on this page.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11. On first launch Windows may show a Defender SmartScreen warning because the installer is new; the Windows installation guide explains how to continue safely.

Do I need an internet connection to use Organizr?

Your data lives on your device, so the app works with your own local data. A connection is needed for sync between devices, for Organizr AI and for the Hevy and FatSecret integrations.

Can I use the same account on phone and computer?

Sync keeps diary entries, completed workouts, meals and Time Analyzr blocks encrypted on the server for 90 days from the event date. Goals expire 90 days after their quarter ends. Complete Wallet history, Board, subscriptions, measurements, recipes, routines and settings are exceptions with no automatic expiry. Expiry removes only the server copy; data stays on your devices.
