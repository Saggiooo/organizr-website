FAQ

About the wallet

Related: habit tracker and workout tracker.

Does Organizr connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection: you add the accounts and transactions you want to track. That is a deliberate choice, and it is why your financial data can stay on your devices.

Can I add transactions with AI instead of typing them in?

Yes. With Quick add with Organizr AI you write or dictate a sentence like “10.50 sandwich cash” and the AI prepares the transaction with amount, description, account, category and date. You check it and confirm before it is saved. It uses Organizr AI credits and reads only your text and the names of your accounts and categories, never balances or history.

Can I track subscriptions?

Yes. The subscription tracker holds every subscription with its renewal date and cost, including shared ones, and shows what they add up to each month.

Is the wallet available on the free plan?

Organizr Lite includes a wallet with one account and up to 6 subscriptions. Pro and Lifetime remove those limits and add the advanced dashboards.

Can I track investments too?

Yes. Investments are tracked alongside your accounts and transactions, so your position and your spending are in the same view.

Where is my financial data stored?

Wallet and Subscriptions have no 90-day limit: complete history stays encrypted on the server to keep balances and reports accurate. Data also stays on your devices.
